# Blender Update Framerate Add-on

Updates your start & end frames and time stretching to match your updated FPS value, compared with your originals.

The settings panel is located under the scene's frame rate settings.

Thanks to the [Auto-Scale FPS add-on](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/auto-scale-fps/?utm_source=blender-5.2.1-lts), which I referenced for the `msgbus` stuff.
